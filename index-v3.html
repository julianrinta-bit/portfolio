The systems

Volume without a system is just noise.

A workflow that tracked every piece from brief to publication, across languages and formats, with handoffs and bottlenecks visible in one place. A two-stage quality control process, non-native to native to spot-check, applied in every market I shipped into. KPI dashboards governing 50+ properties on click-through, retention and revenue per channel. That is how the bar held in markets where nobody on the leadership team spoke the language.

The proof

It survives the transplant.

A translation dies in the new market. A format is rebuilt natively and lives. Four markets opened from zero in eighteen months at TheSoul, past 20M followers. Then Bored Panda from one property to more than fifty, across fifteen languages. Same logic each time, different market, built from the ground up in each one.

Gotcha!, as published in each market. Same design language, rebuilt market by market rather than captioned. Seven of them here; there were fifty-seven when I left.

The repeat

The outlier repeats.

One spike is luck, or it was paid. Strategy shows up as outliers that keep arriving. Gotcha! reached number one worldwide in its category, across 57 channels in fifteen languages. Crafty Panda reached number one worldwide on Facebook, with 2.1 billion views in a single month. Different brands, different platforms, the same hand.

The same discipline works where the unit is pipeline rather than views: a B2B software funnel I rebuilt went from 1,200 to 12,000 subscribers and produced 85 qualified demos a month, roughly $380K in pipeline.
